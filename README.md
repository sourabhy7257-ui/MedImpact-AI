# MedImpact AI

## Evidence-Verified Medical Intervention & Outcome Analysis

MedImpact AI is a Data Science and AI project designed to analyze the impact of medical interventions using evidence verification and longitudinal outcome analysis.

### Core Principle

> No evidence, no confident medical claim.

## Objectives

- Analyze baseline medical data
- Retrieve relevant medical evidence
- Verify AI-generated medical claims
- Track medical interventions
- Compare pre-intervention and post-intervention outcomes
- Perform statistical analysis
- Distinguish observed changes from causal effects
- Generate evidence-backed impact reports

## Initial Case Study

The first implementation focuses on diabetes management using HbA1c as the primary outcome variable.

## System Workflow

User / Clinical Dataset  
↓

↓

↓  
Claim Verification  
↓  
AI-Assisted Intervention  
↓  
Follow-up Data  
↓  
Before vs After Analysis  
↓  
Statistical Analysis  
↓  
Impact Report

## Safety & Scope

MedImpact AI is an experimental research and educational project.

It is not intended to diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

The system reports observed changes and statistical associations. Causal conclusions require appropriate study design and assumptions.
